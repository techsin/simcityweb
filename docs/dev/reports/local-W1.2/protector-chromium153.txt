T034b correction, Nov 3: Chromium 153's V8 (15.3) HAS the ArrayBufferDetaching protector, and tracks array buffer views.
The fix round concluded from experiments 7 and 8 below that it had none. Both detached `new ArrayBuffer(8)`, a buffer with no
view, which this V8 does not count: a detach of a buffer with no view, or with exactly one TypedArray view, updates that view
in place and leaves the protector intact (a single-view transfer is free). It is invalidated, for good and per isolate, by a
detach of a buffer with two or more views (of one kind or two), a DataView, or a detach key. Experiment 9 shows the views
and the DataView (a detach key is the review's reading of V8's source: script cannot make one, and a wasm memory.grow is not
one here). tools/qa-worker.mjs's 'control' step now detaches a buffer with two TypedArray views (positive) and one with no view
(negative). Keeping the protector means never detaching a buffer with two or more views, a DataView or a detach key; a wasm
memory.grow counts by the old buffer's views like any other detach (the kernels make several views of their heap, so a grow
of it would invalidate the protector).
